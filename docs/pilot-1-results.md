# Pilot 1 — Harry Whyte, "Next 7 Days"

Run 2026-09-11 against live Close data. Five leads, all eligible, all
confirmed to have happened via the CRM-state gate.

## Deliverables

| Artifact | Location |
|---|---|
| Tracker (25 columns × 5 rows) | Google Sheet "Closer Deep Dive Tracker — Harry Whyte" |
| Narrative + stats report | Published HTML artifact |

## Patterns

1. **The close-out text is a template.** All five got a near-identical
   "appreciate your time today + resources and agreement sent" SMS within
   two hours. Kevin's carries a paste artifact ("as well as What agreement").
2. **The chase lands on day 4–5,** consistently: Will 4d, Ming 5d,
   Anthony 5d. The vehicle is a personal Loom video.
3. **Response speed is bimodal.** Inside a live thread: 3–15 minutes.
   Across a dormant gap: 4–5 days. The median hides both.
4. **Send hours need a timezone caveat.** Harry was working from Brazil
   (GMT−3) during this window; an 11:43pm ET send is just past midnight
   local, not an all-nighter.

## Measurement notes

- Lags are from **scheduled end** (`starts_at + 45m`), not real call end.
  Anthony's call overran to 71m51s, so his "+25m" first touch actually
  landed during the call. Overrunning calls read high.
- Ari and Kevin were 1–2 days post-call at extraction. Their thin threads
  are recency, not neglect.
